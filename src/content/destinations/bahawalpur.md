Bahawalpur is a historic city in southern Punjab, once the capital of a princely state and known for its palaces and its university. It is a regional hub for surrounding farming towns, and has long-standing links with the UAE through families working in the Gulf.

## Shipping to Bahawalpur from the UAE

We collect from anywhere in the UAE, pack and document the goods at our Jebel Ali warehouse, and send them to Pakistan. Cargo is cleared at the port of entry and forwarded by road to Bahawalpur.

Model Town, Satellite Town and Civil Lines are residential areas that are easy to reach, while Farid Gate sits closer to the old city. Nearby towns such as Ahmedpur East and Baghdad ul Jadeed are also within our delivery area.
