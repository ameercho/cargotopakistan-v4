Faisalabad is Pakistan's textile capital and one of its largest industrial cities. Alongside the usual household shipments, we regularly handle cargo for small traders and manufacturers who buy or sell between the UAE and Faisalabad.

## Shipping to Faisalabad from the UAE

Your cargo is collected from your address in the UAE and brought to our Jebel Ali warehouse, where it is packed and documented. It is then sent to Pakistan, cleared at the port of entry and carried by road to Faisalabad.

## Delivery in Faisalabad

Residential areas such as D Ground, Madina Town, People's Colony and Gulberg are easy to reach by road, while Susan Road and Ghulam Muhammad Abad have a mix of homes and businesses. We coordinate final delivery through Local Transport, Leopards Courier, TCS and dedicated transport services, using dedicated transport for bulky or commercial consignments and a courier for smaller parcels. If you are sending goods for a business, let us know in advance so we can prepare the right documents.
