Gujranwala is a major industrial and manufacturing city in Punjab, and it sits on the main road between Lahore and Islamabad. People sending cargo there range from families in Model Town to traders in the city's busy markets.

## Shipping to Gujranwala from the UAE

We collect from anywhere in the UAE, pack and document the cargo at our Jebel Ali warehouse, and send it to Pakistan. Goods are cleared at the port of entry and forwarded by road to Gujranwala.

## Delivery in Gujranwala

Housing schemes such as Wapda Town, Satellite Town, Model Town and People's Colony are straightforward to reach. Civil Lines and Rehman Pura are older and busier. We coordinate final delivery through Local Transport, Leopards Courier, TCS and dedicated transport services, using dedicated transport for larger loads and courier for smaller parcels. Since Gujranwala lies close to Lahore and Sialkot, it can also be useful to tell us if the same shipment has parts going to several cities.
