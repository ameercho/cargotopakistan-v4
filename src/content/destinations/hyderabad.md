Hyderabad is the second largest city in Sindh and sits on the Indus, not far from Karachi. Many residents have family in the UAE, and cargo from the Gulf is a regular feature of household life in areas such as Latifabad and Qasimabad.

## Shipping to Hyderabad from the UAE

We collect from your address in the UAE, pack and document the goods at our Jebel Ali warehouse, and send them to Pakistan. Since Hyderabad is close to Karachi, goods are cleared at the port of entry and then have a comparatively short onward journey.

## Delivery in Hyderabad

Latifabad and Qasimabad are large residential areas that are straightforward to reach. Hirabad and Hussainabad are more central, and Thandi Sarak and the Cantonment are well-known landmarks that help with directions. We coordinate final delivery through Local Transport, Leopards Courier, TCS and dedicated transport services and choose the option that suits your shipment and address. Please give us the receiver's phone number and a nearby landmark when you book.
