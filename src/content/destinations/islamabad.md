Islamabad is Pakistan's federal capital and a planned city laid out in lettered sectors. Many of the people we ship for here are families with relatives in the UAE, diplomats and professionals relocating, and small businesses in the sector markets.

## Shipping to Islamabad from the UAE

We collect your cargo from anywhere in the UAE, prepare the paperwork at our Jebel Ali warehouse, and send it to Pakistan. Islamabad is inland, so your goods are cleared at the port of entry and then forwarded by road to the capital.

## Delivery across the sectors

Islamabad's sector grid makes addresses easy to locate: F-6, F-7, F-8, F-10, F-11 and G-9 are all within our regular delivery area. Because the city is close to Rawalpindi, a single delivery run can often cover both. Final delivery is coordinated through Local Transport, Leopards Courier, TCS and dedicated transport services, matched to the size of the shipment and the address. Include the sector, street and house number on the booking so the driver can find the door first time.
