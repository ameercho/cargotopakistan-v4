Lahore is the capital of Punjab and Pakistan's cultural heart, and one of the most common destinations for cargo leaving the UAE. Families in Gulberg, Model Town and Johar Town regularly receive household goods, clothing and gifts from relatives working in Dubai and Sharjah.

## Sending cargo from the UAE to Lahore

Your shipment is collected from your address in the UAE, packed and documented at our Jebel Ali warehouse, and forwarded to Pakistan. Since Lahore is an inland city, goods are cleared at the port of entry and then carried onward by road.

## Delivery within Lahore

Lahore's planned neighbourhoods, from Defence and Cantt to Garden Town, make door delivery straightforward once the cargo reaches the city. Larger consignments such as furniture or full household moves normally travel by dedicated transport, while smaller boxes can be passed to a courier. We work with Local Transport, Leopards Courier, TCS and dedicated transport services, and assign the one that fits your goods and your address.
