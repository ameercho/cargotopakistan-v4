Larkana is a historic city in Sindh, close to the ancient site of Mohenjo-daro and the town of Naudero. It is a regional centre for the surrounding farming districts, and cargo from the UAE is a regular way for families to send goods home.

## Shipping to Larkana from the UAE

We collect from your address in the UAE, pack and document the cargo at our Jebel Ali warehouse, and send it to Pakistan. Goods are cleared at the port of entry and then forwarded by road to Larkana.

## Delivery in Larkana and nearby towns

Within the city, Station Road, Jinnah Bagh and Civil Hospital Road are the main reference points for addresses. Nearby towns such as Naudero, Bakrani and Warrah are also within our delivery area. We coordinate final delivery through Local Transport, Leopards Courier, TCS and dedicated transport services, choosing one to suit the size of your load. If the address is outside the city, send us the full address and the nearest town so we can check the delivery arrangement before dispatch.
