Multan, known as the City of Saints, is one of the oldest cities in South Asia and the commercial centre of southern Punjab. Many families there have relatives in the UAE, and cargo is a regular way of staying connected.

## Shipping to Multan from the UAE

We collect from your door in the UAE, pack and document the cargo at our Jebel Ali warehouse, and send it to Pakistan. It is cleared at the port of entry and then forwarded by road to Multan.

## Delivery in Multan

Gulgasht Colony, Model Town, Garden Town and Mumtazabad are residential areas where a delivery vehicle can reach the door directly. Around Shah Rukn-e-Alam and other parts of the old city, streets are narrower and deliveries need more planning. We coordinate final delivery through Local Transport, Leopards Courier, TCS and dedicated transport services and choose between them according to your goods and address. A landmark and the receiver's phone number on the booking help a great deal.
