Peshawar is the capital of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and one of the oldest continuously inhabited cities in the region. Many households in Peshawar have family members working in the UAE, and sending goods home is a regular routine.

## Sending cargo from the UAE to Peshawar

Your shipment is collected in the UAE, packed and documented at our Jebel Ali warehouse, and sent to Pakistan. Peshawar is a long way inland, so goods are cleared at the port of entry and then forwarded north by road.

## Delivery in Peshawar

Hayatabad and University Town are planned neighbourhoods that are easy for a vehicle to reach. Saddar, Gulbahar and the areas around the old city are busier and have narrow approaches. Cantt and Warsak Road are more open. Final delivery is coordinated through Local Transport, Leopards Courier, TCS and dedicated transport services, and we match the choice to the size of the load and the address. Giving us a contact number for the person receiving the goods in Peshawar makes delivery much smoother.
