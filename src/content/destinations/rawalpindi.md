Rawalpindi is the twin city of Islamabad and a busy commercial centre in its own right, with crowded markets, a large cantonment and many families who have relatives working in the Gulf. Cargo from the UAE is a regular part of life here.

## Shipping to Rawalpindi from the UAE

We arrange collection anywhere in the Emirates, then pack and document the shipment at our Jebel Ali warehouse before sending it to Pakistan. Goods are cleared at the port of entry and carried by road to Rawalpindi.

## Delivery around Rawalpindi

Areas such as Saddar, Committee Chowk and Commercial Market have narrow streets and heavy traffic, so we plan each delivery around the address. Cantt, Westridge and Chaklala are more spread out and suit larger vehicles. Final delivery is coordinated through Local Transport, Leopards Courier, TCS and dedicated transport services, with the choice depending on the size of your consignment. Give us a nearby landmark along with the address when you book; it helps drivers a great deal in older parts of the city.
