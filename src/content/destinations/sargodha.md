Sargodha, known as the City of Eagles, is a city in central Punjab famous for its citrus orchards and its air base. It is a busy regional market, and many families there receive cargo from relatives in the UAE.

## Shipping to Sargodha from the UAE

Your cargo is collected from your address in the UAE, packed and documented at our Jebel Ali warehouse, and sent to Pakistan. It is cleared at the port of entry and forwarded by road to Sargodha.

We coordinate final delivery through Local Transport, Leopards Courier, TCS and dedicated transport services, choosing the option that suits the size of your shipment.
