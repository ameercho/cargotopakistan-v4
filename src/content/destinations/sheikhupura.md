Sheikhupura is a historic and industrial city close to Lahore, on the main road towards Faisalabad. It is known for Hiran Minar and for its factories, and it has many families with members working in the UAE.

## Shipping to Sheikhupura from the UAE

We collect from anywhere in the Emirates, pack and document the cargo at our Jebel Ali warehouse, and send it to Pakistan. Goods are cleared at the port of entry and forwarded by road to Sheikhupura.

## Delivery in Sheikhupura

Civil Lines, Model Town and Satellite Town are residential, while Railway Road and GT Road are the main commercial routes. Cantt is quieter and more open. We coordinate final delivery through Local Transport, Leopards Courier, TCS and dedicated transport services and select the option that suits your goods. Because Sheikhupura is near Lahore, we can also deliver to both places from a single shipment if you give us both addresses.
