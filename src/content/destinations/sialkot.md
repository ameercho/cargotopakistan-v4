Sialkot is best known around the world for sports goods and surgical instruments, and it is a city with strong business links to the UAE. Alongside household cargo, we often handle samples, tools and commercial consignments going in both directions.

## Sending cargo from the UAE to Sialkot

Your goods are collected in the UAE, packed and documented at our Jebel Ali warehouse, and sent on to Pakistan. They are cleared at the port of entry and forwarded by road to Sialkot.

## Delivery in Sialkot

Residential areas such as Model Town and Cantt are straightforward to reach, while Paris Road and Kashmir Road are busy commercial streets. The Small Industrial Estate and Zafarwal Road suit larger vehicles and commercial deliveries. We coordinate final delivery through Local Transport, Leopards Courier, TCS and dedicated transport services, and pick the one that fits the size and nature of the consignment. If your goods are for a factory or workshop, tell us in advance so we can plan the drop-off.
