Sukkur is an important commercial centre in northern Sindh, on the Indus and close to the famous Sukkur Barrage. It serves as a hub for trade across the region, and many households there have members working in the UAE.

## Shipping to Sukkur from the UAE

Your cargo is collected from anywhere in the UAE, packed and documented at our Jebel Ali warehouse, and sent to Pakistan. It is cleared at the port of entry and then forwarded by road to Sukkur.

## Delivery in Sukkur

Sukkur is split between the main city and New Sukkur across the river, so the address matters. Civil Lines, Barrage Colony and Labour Colony are residential, while Station Road and Minara Road are busy commercial streets. We coordinate final delivery through Local Transport, Leopards Courier, TCS and dedicated transport services, and pick one according to the size of your consignment. Please state clearly whether the address is in Sukkur or New Sukkur when you book.
