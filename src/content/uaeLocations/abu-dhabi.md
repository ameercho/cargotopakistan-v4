If you live in Abu Dhabi and need to send cargo to Pakistan, the main question is convenience: the capital is spread across islands, suburbs and industrial zones, and getting to a cargo office can take half a day. We come to you instead, collecting from your home, office or shop and arranging delivery to your recipient in Pakistan.

## Free pickup coverage in Abu Dhabi

Pickup is free from locations within our Abu Dhabi coverage area. That includes the island communities of **Al Reem Island, Yas Island and Saadiyat Island**, the suburbs of **Al Raha, Khalifa City, Al Reef, Al Shamkha and Al Nahyan**, the industrial district of **Mussafah**, and **Baniyas**. If your area isn't listed here, send us your location and we will confirm whether we can collect from it.

## Door-to-door from Abu Dhabi to Pakistan

Our [door-to-door cargo service](/services/door-to-door/) covers the whole journey. We collect from your address, pack and document the shipment at our Jebel Ali warehouse, handle customs clearance, and send it on to Pakistan, where it is delivered to the receiver's door through Local Transport, Leopards Courier, TCS and dedicated transport services. We deliver to [Karachi](/pakistan-cargo-to-karachi/), [Lahore](/pakistan-cargo-to-lahore/), [Islamabad](/pakistan-cargo-to-islamabad/), [Peshawar](/pakistan-cargo-to-peshawar/), [Faisalabad](/pakistan-cargo-to-faisalabad/) and [every other major Pakistani city](/service-areas/).
