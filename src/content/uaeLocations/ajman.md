Ajman is compact and densely populated, and most residents are a short drive from the Sharjah and Dubai borders. For people sending cargo from Ajman to Pakistan, the simplest option is to have it collected from home or work and delivered straight to the receiver in Pakistan.

## Free pickup coverage in Ajman

Pickup is free from locations within our Ajman coverage area. That includes the residential neighbourhoods of **Al Nuaimiya, Al Rashidiya, Al Rawda, Al Zahya, Al Hamidiya and Garden City**, central **Ajman Downtown**, the industrial and commercial area of **Al Jurf**, and **Al Mowaihat and Mushairef**. If your address is somewhere else in Ajman, send us your location and we will confirm whether it is covered.

## Door-to-door from Ajman to Pakistan

Our [door-to-door cargo service](/services/door-to-door/) covers the whole journey. We collect from your address, pack and document the shipment at our Jebel Ali warehouse, handle customs clearance, and send it on to Pakistan, where it is delivered to the receiver's door through Local Transport, Leopards Courier, TCS and dedicated transport services. We deliver to [Karachi](/pakistan-cargo-to-karachi/), [Lahore](/pakistan-cargo-to-lahore/), [Islamabad](/pakistan-cargo-to-islamabad/), [Peshawar](/pakistan-cargo-to-peshawar/), [Faisalabad](/pakistan-cargo-to-faisalabad/) and [every other major Pakistani city](/service-areas/).
