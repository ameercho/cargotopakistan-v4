Al Ain is the UAE's inland Garden City, roughly 150 km from the coast and on the Oman border. Because there is no nearby port or cargo terminal, people sending cargo from Al Ain to Pakistan often face a long drive just to hand it over. We collect from Al Ain itself and take care of everything afterwards.

## Free pickup coverage in Al Ain

Pickup is free from locations within our Al Ain coverage area. That includes **Al Muwaiji, Al Jimi, Al Mutawaa, Al Khurair, Al Markhaniya, Al Maqam, Al Bateen, Al Sarooj and Al Towayya** across the city, and **Hili** on the border side. We collect from homes, offices and farms. If you are in a village or district outside this list, send us your location and we will confirm whether we can reach it.

## Door-to-door from Al Ain to Pakistan

Our [door-to-door cargo service](/services/door-to-door/) covers the whole journey. We collect from your address, pack and document the shipment at our Jebel Ali warehouse, handle customs clearance, and send it on to Pakistan, where it is delivered to the receiver's door through Local Transport, Leopards Courier, TCS and dedicated transport services. We deliver to [Karachi](/pakistan-cargo-to-karachi/), [Lahore](/pakistan-cargo-to-lahore/), [Islamabad](/pakistan-cargo-to-islamabad/), [Peshawar](/pakistan-cargo-to-peshawar/), [Faisalabad](/pakistan-cargo-to-faisalabad/) and [every other major Pakistani city](/service-areas/).
