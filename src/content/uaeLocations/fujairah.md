Fujairah is the UAE's only emirate entirely on the Gulf of Oman coast, separated from the main cargo hubs by the Hajar Mountains. That distance is why cargo from Fujairah to Pakistan is easier when someone comes to you. We collect on the east coast and handle the rest of the journey.

## Free pickup coverage in Fujairah

Pickup is free from locations within our Fujairah coverage area. That includes **Fujairah City, Al Faseel, Mirbah and Dadna**, the coastal towns of **Dibba, Qidfa, Al Bidya and Al Aqah**, and the mountain-road town of **Masafi**, as well as **Al Halah**. If your address is elsewhere on the east coast, send us your location and we will confirm whether it is covered.

## Door-to-door from Fujairah to Pakistan

Our [door-to-door cargo service](/services/door-to-door/) covers the whole journey. We collect from your address, pack and document the shipment at our Jebel Ali warehouse, handle customs clearance, and send it on to Pakistan, where it is delivered to the receiver's door through Local Transport, Leopards Courier, TCS and dedicated transport services. We deliver to [Karachi](/pakistan-cargo-to-karachi/), [Lahore](/pakistan-cargo-to-lahore/), [Islamabad](/pakistan-cargo-to-islamabad/), [Peshawar](/pakistan-cargo-to-peshawar/), [Faisalabad](/pakistan-cargo-to-faisalabad/) and [every other major Pakistani city](/service-areas/).
