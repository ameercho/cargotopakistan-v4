Ras Al Khaimah is the UAE's northernmost emirate and is some distance from the main cargo hubs of Dubai and Sharjah. For people sending cargo from RAK to Pakistan, collection at the door saves a long trip south. We collect from your address in RAK, then pack, document and dispatch the shipment.

## Free pickup coverage in Ras Al Khaimah

Pickup is free from locations within our RAK coverage area. That includes the central neighbourhoods of **Al Nakheel, Al Qusaidat, Al Dhait, Al Mairid, Al Riffa, Khuzam and Al Mamourah**, the coastal areas of **Al Hamra and Al Jazirah Al Hamra**, and **Al Rams** in the north. We collect from homes, offices and shops. If your address is not on this list, send us your location and we will confirm.

## Door-to-door from Ras Al Khaimah to Pakistan

Our [door-to-door cargo service](/services/door-to-door/) covers the whole journey. We collect from your address, pack and document the shipment at our Jebel Ali warehouse, handle customs clearance, and send it on to Pakistan, where it is delivered to the receiver's door through Local Transport, Leopards Courier, TCS and dedicated transport services. We deliver to [Karachi](/pakistan-cargo-to-karachi/), [Lahore](/pakistan-cargo-to-lahore/), [Islamabad](/pakistan-cargo-to-islamabad/), [Peshawar](/pakistan-cargo-to-peshawar/), [Faisalabad](/pakistan-cargo-to-faisalabad/) and [every other major Pakistani city](/service-areas/).
