Many people searching for cargo from Sharjah to Pakistan live in dense residential districts close to the Dubai border, where parking is tight and travelling to a cargo office is a chore. We collect from your door instead, then pack, document and send your shipment on to Pakistan.

## Free pickup from anywhere in Sharjah

We collect from anywhere in Sharjah. Common pickup areas include the busy residential districts of **Al Nahda, Al Taawun, Al Qasimia and Al Yarmook**, the waterfront areas of **Al Majaz and Al Khan**, the newer communities of **Aljada and Muwaileh**, and the commercial areas of **Al Ghuwair, Abu Shagara and Al Nabba**. Whatever your area in Sharjah, send us your location and we will arrange the pickup.

## Door-to-door from Sharjah to Pakistan

Our [door-to-door cargo service](/services/door-to-door/) covers the whole journey. We collect from your address, pack and document the shipment at our Jebel Ali warehouse, handle customs clearance, and send it on to Pakistan, where it is delivered to the receiver's door through Local Transport, Leopards Courier, TCS and dedicated transport services. We deliver to [Karachi](/pakistan-cargo-to-karachi/), [Lahore](/pakistan-cargo-to-lahore/), [Islamabad](/pakistan-cargo-to-islamabad/), [Peshawar](/pakistan-cargo-to-peshawar/), [Faisalabad](/pakistan-cargo-to-faisalabad/) and [every other major Pakistani city](/service-areas/).
