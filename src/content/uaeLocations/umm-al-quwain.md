Umm Al Quwain is the UAE's smallest and quietest emirate, with few cargo options close at hand. People sending cargo from UAQ to Pakistan would otherwise have to travel to Sharjah or Dubai. We collect from your address and take care of everything afterwards.

## Free pickup coverage in Umm Al Quwain

Pickup is free from locations within our UAQ coverage area. That includes **Umm Al Quwain City, Al Salamah, Al Raas, Al Rafa, Al Riqqah, Al Maidan and Al Khor**, **Falaj Al Mualla** inland, and the waterfront areas of **UAQ Marina and Dreamland**. If your address is not on this list, send us your location and we will confirm whether it is covered.

## Door-to-door from Umm Al Quwain to Pakistan

Our [door-to-door cargo service](/services/door-to-door/) covers the whole journey. We collect from your address, pack and document the shipment at our Jebel Ali warehouse, handle customs clearance, and send it on to Pakistan, where it is delivered to the receiver's door through Local Transport, Leopards Courier, TCS and dedicated transport services. We deliver to [Karachi](/pakistan-cargo-to-karachi/), [Lahore](/pakistan-cargo-to-lahore/), [Islamabad](/pakistan-cargo-to-islamabad/), [Peshawar](/pakistan-cargo-to-peshawar/), [Faisalabad](/pakistan-cargo-to-faisalabad/) and [every other major Pakistani city](/service-areas/).
